Questions

Quick answers

Can we add a believer without a family?

No. Every believer belongs to a family, and a family is created together with its head. This keeps your records connected and your counts accurate.

What happens when the family head needs to be removed?

You choose a replacement first. The chosen member becomes the new head and the original head moves to Trash.

Can we get deleted records back?

Yes. Deleted believers wait in Trash until you restore them or delete them forever. Trashed records are left out of reports and analytics.

What can we export?

Any filtered report can be exported as an Excel file or an A4 landscape PDF with a cover summary page, including the birthday and anniversary reminder lists.

Do we need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser, and the left-hand menu takes you to each module.
